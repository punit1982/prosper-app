# GROW v5.1 — ANNEX B · SPECIAL MEASUREMENT BASES
**Load when the assigned basis is M2, M3, M4, M5 or M9. Read only the assigned section.**

> **The rule that governs all of them.** A basis is assigned by its stated trigger and by nothing else — never by theme, never by sector, never by which cluster the name sits in. **And a basis whose mandated checks the company does not disclose is not eligible for that company: fall back to M1 and print `M?→M1 FALLBACK — checks not disclosed`.** A rule that cannot be computed cannot govern.

---

## M2 · BALANCE-SHEET LENDER
**Trigger: lending assets exceed 25% of total assets.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Margin room runs on** | **Return on tangible common equity** against the company's own ten-year range and its stated target. The mechanism is the efficiency ratio, and it must be named and dated like any other |
| **Growth runs on** | Revenue, with the **cycle-base guard almost always applying** — check whether the trailing rate is a credit-cycle peak rather than a run-rate |
| **Cash test** | **Pre-provision operating profit against net income**, and **net charge-offs against the provision.** A bank earning its way through a released reserve is not earning |
| **Mandatory retrieval** | Loan book by vintage · non-performing ratio and coverage · deposit mix and cost · capital ratio against requirement · the through-cycle loss rate of a named seasoned comparable |
| **Standing rules** | **A loan book is not a forward book.** Above 50% growth in twelve months it is unseasoned by construction, its own arrears rate is not evidence, and criterion 7 is scored net of a named comparable's through-cycle loss rate. **Deposits funding it are a liability, not traction.** **Cash inside the licensed entity is not the group's cash** unless the filing states a distributable surplus |

## M3 · CAPACITY AND UTILISATION
**Trigger: fixed assets exceed 40% of total assets, or the business sells capacity.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Margin room runs on** | **Unit economics at full utilisation versus today** — cost per megawatt, per wafer, per twenty-foot equivalent, per rig-day. The mechanism is utilisation, mix or a named debottleneck, with a date |
| **Growth runs on** | **Revenue and volume, both printed.** Revenue alone hides price |
| **Cash test** | Operating cash flow against net income, **plus the maintenance-versus-growth capital-spending split.** A business whose maintenance capital spending is not disclosed cannot be assessed on this basis — fall back to M1 |
| **Mandatory retrieval** | Installed and contracted capacity in units · utilisation and its trend · cost per unit and its trend · the capital cost per unit of new capacity · asset life against contract life |
| **Standing rule** | Where capacity is contracted, **the contracted share, the average remaining term and counterparty credit are retrieved before criterion 7 is scored.** Uncontracted capacity is optionality, not traction |

---

## M4 · REAL ESTATE AND NET ASSET VALUE
**Trigger: development or investment property exceeds 50% of assets.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Margin room runs on** | **Development margin and yield on cost** against the company's own range. The mechanism is a mix shift from trading to recurring income, named and dated |
| **Growth runs on** | **Recurring income, never total revenue.** Trading gains are lumpy and are not a growth signal |
| **Cash test** | Collections against recognised revenue, and backlog conversion |
| **Mandatory retrieval** | Net asset value per share and the valuer's date · the yield used and its spread to the local risk-free rate · development pipeline with completion dates · pre-sales or pre-lets · debt secured against which assets, and whether recourse |
| **Standing rule** | **A net asset value is a valuation, not a fact.** Print who produced it, on what date, at what yield, and what a 100bp yield move does to it |

---

## M5 · RESOURCE AND PRICE-TAKER
**Trigger: sells a quoted commodity at spot.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Margin room runs on** | **Unit cash cost against the industry cost curve.** Position on the curve is the whole analysis. The mechanism is grade, strip ratio or recovery, each with a date |
| **Growth runs on** | **Volume, never revenue.** Revenue is a price artefact |
| **Cash test** | Operating cash flow against net income **at a mid-cycle price, never at spot** |
| **Mandatory retrieval** | Reserve and resource statement with its date and the price assumption behind it · life of asset · all-in sustaining cost · position on the published cost curve · hedge book |
| **Standing rule** | **Mid-cycle normalisation is mandatory here, and spot-price forward earnings are forbidden.** The mid-cycle price is named, sourced and dated |

## M9 · COMPOSITE — SUM OF THE PARTS
**Trigger: Gate 0 fired — no single segment is more than 60% of value, and the segments would classify under different archetypes.**

**The method, in order:**

1. **Split by reported segment**, using the company's own segment note. Where the note does not permit a split, this basis is not eligible — fall back to M1 and print the fallback.
2. **Classify every segment on its own archetype and its own measurement basis.** Print the table.
3. **Value every segment on its own primary method**, at its own required return.
4. **Sum, then subtract:** unallocated corporate cost capitalised at the group's blended rate, net debt held at the centre, and any pension or legacy liability.
5. **Apply a holding-company discount, stated as a number with its basis** — a named observed transaction, the company's own history of realisations, or the trading discount of a named comparable holding company. **A discount asserted without one of those three is Class C and counts against the driving-inputs limit.**
6. **Print what the largest segment alone is worth**, and what share of the total value it carries.

**Durability uses profile P4 and is scored on the group.** Criterion 3 is scored on **the weakest segment that carries more than 20% of value**, not on the average — a conglomerate is only as durable as the part that can break it.

**The standing question, printed:** *is the sum of the parts a valuation or a plan?* A discount closes only where management has actually separated something. **Where no separation has been announced, the base case holds the discount constant and the Bull case closes it.**
