## C1 · The probability engine

**Pinned at adoption and carried unchanged. Never re-retrieved per run — that was the source of the variance.** Pinned against the BIO/QLS Clinical Development Success Rates series and Wong, Siah & Lo in *Biostatistics*.

| Transition | Rate |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 → 2 | 62.5% |
| Phase 2 → 3 | 32.5% |
| Phase 3 → filing | 60.0% |
| Filing → approval | 87.5% |

**By area, Phase 1 → approval:** haematology 23% · rare/orphan 22.5% · infectious 17.5% · metabolic 15% · immunology 10% · CNS 8% · **oncology 5%**.

**Chain every transition the asset still has to clear, then apply the modifiers.** A Phase 3 asset uses 60.0% × 87.5% = 52.5% before modifiers.

**Modifiers, multiplied together, their product bounded to [0.4×, 2.0×]:**

| Modifier | × |
|---|---|
| Mechanism validated by human genetics, or an approved drug on the same target | 1.4 |
| **Same molecule already approved by a stringent regulator elsewhere, same indication** | 1.6 |
| Endpoint has precedent — an approved drug won on it here | 1.2 |
| Biomarker-selected or enriched population | 1.5 |
| **Registrational data from a population with materially different standard of care** | 0.6 |
| Powered on an effect size at or above the best ever seen in the class | 0.7 |
| Sponsor has never completed a registrational trial | 0.85 |

## C2 · From one probability to four cases

```
fails            = 1 − p
works + partly   = p,  split 60 / 40 by default
```

**The 60/40 split is the only default here, and it exists because approval does not guarantee commercial success.** Move it only against a named fact — a comparator's actual launch curve, a payer decision, a pricing precedent — and say in the memo what moved it.

*Worked: a Phase 3 asset at 60% × 87.5% = 52.5%, modified ×1.6 (approved elsewhere, same indication) ×1.2 (endpoint precedent) ×0.6 (different background therapy) = **60.5%** → fails 39.5%, works 36.3%, partly works 24.2%.*

## C3 · Two standing rules

**An undisclosed licensee royalty defaults to 15% of net sales plus 20% of gross milestones, stated in the call.**

**Any therapeutic losing exclusivity inside the horizon must have the cliff modelled explicitly**, with the entry year and decay printed. A terminal value that grows through a patent cliff is an error.

## C4 · Retrieval, by archetype

Lead asset, mechanism, modality · **originator or licensee, and if licensee the royalty and milestones** · every programme with phase and next dated event · runway **to the next value event plus two years** · **patent expiry, data exclusivity, and the expected generic or biosimilar year** · the named approved comparator and its net price · **three stage-matched deal comparables** · regulatory designations with dates.

**H2 additionally:** trial geography, background therapy, endpoint precedent, powering against the best in class, prior placebo response.
**H3 additionally:** **the launch curve against a named analogue**, gross-to-net, payer coverage, persistence, **the loss-of-exclusivity year and the modelled decay**.
**H4 additionally:** installed base, **utilisation per placement**, consumable attach, reimbursement code and rate, replacement cycle, any recall.
